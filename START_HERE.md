# Start here

1. You are running code on **your own Windows PC**. The coding agent cannot see your PC or iPhone.
2. Open `NEXT_STEPS.md` in this folder and follow its numbered steps exactly. Stage 1 needs no
   administrator rights and changes nothing on the PC or the phone.
3. When it asks, paste the reviewed contents of `SHARE_BACK.md` into the agent conversation.
   Keep everything else (the private report, `config.local.json` and the future `mitmproxy` folder) on the PC.

Phone safety reminder (for later stages): if the iPhone's internet ever stops working during a test,
go to **Settings > Wi-Fi > (i) next to your network > Configure Proxy > Off > Save**.
